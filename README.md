# simple-interest-calculator
A Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a basic program that calculates the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

**Simple Interest (SI):**

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

* **P** = Principal amount
* **R** = Rate of interest per year
* **T** = Time period in years

**Total Amount:**

Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## Example

If:

* Principal = ₹10,000
* Rate = 5% per year
* Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000

**Total Amount = ₹11,000**
